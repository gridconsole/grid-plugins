PASS if plan.md has a `## Approach and options` section with two or three real ways to do the work, each with its trade-off (what it gains and what it costs), and names the chosen one with the reason it was chosen; a choice left to the person counts when it names its Q-n and the recommended option.
FAIL if there is only one option, a second option is a strawman no one would pick (it only exists to lose, or its trade-off is all cost), the options carry no trade-off, or no choice or recommendation is given.
