# Delivering

Reached through the SessionStart index and `grid brain search --topic delivering <words>`; no hook puts it in front of an agent (topics/README.md). Each pointer says what to open and why.

- docs/delivery.md: shows what grid deliver refuses (missing proof, empty plans, closed or stopped tasks), so you do not meet the refusal at the end.
- grid brain keep: lists the proposals that wait; they do not block grid deliver, which posts a curator Job for them after the delivery is written.
- grid proof index: rebuilds the proof index, so every plan item shows proven or not before you claim the work is done.
