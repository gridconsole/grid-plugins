# S2: a Job for a person, with dependencies

## What changed
- `internal/job/job.go`: `RolePerson`, `Job.IsPerson()`, `Job.PersonName()`. The person's name is kept in `intent.owner`, because `job-record.json` allows no extra field and `contract/schemas` is outside this lane.
- `internal/orchestrator/ready.go`: a role Person Job is taken out of `Ready.Ready` (so `job next` and `Tick` never assign it to a worker) and listed as waiting with "waiting for <who>". Jobs behind it stay held by the existing `after` rule (dependency met only when the Job is accepted). Jobs that do not depend on it are offered as before.
- `internal/orchestrator/person.go`: `AnswerPerson` writes a plain `post` message (who, when, the answer) and a `control` accepted record that follows it. It does not check who the caller is. Refuses an empty answer, a Job that is not a person's, and one already closed.
- `internal/cli/orchestrator.go`: `grid job post --person <who>` (sets role Person and `intent.owner`, type job). `grid job result <J> --body '{"answer":"..."}'` on a Person Job goes to `AnswerPerson` under the task lease (a person's Job has no worker lease). Gate code, `refuseAnswerPost` and the feed person layer are untouched.
- `contract/commands.json`: `--person` added to every `job` verb entry that shares the flag set. I edited it by hand because the regenerate command was denied in this session; `TestContractCommandsParserInventory` passes against it. Please regenerate with `make generate` to confirm it is byte-identical.
- New tests: `internal/orchestrator/person_test.go`, `internal/cli/person_job_test.go`.
