## Not done, on purpose

- The four paired roles (builder, checker, curator, reviewer) still have a body in each folder. Making the template body a copy of `agents/<role>.md` would drop template text that `TestWmTpl*` and `TestSaBrain*` tests read (proof-add spans, the decide line) and would collide with rows 4, 5, 10 and 11 of the same review, which other lanes edit in those templates. `docs/agents.md:8` records that which of the two sets goes is the owner's decision. `TestRoleCopiesAgree` pins the facts the copies disagreed on instead. Rows 4, 5, 10 and 11 are untouched.
