## What changed

- `internal/orchestrator/pg_closed_task.go`: the pass for a closed task now keeps a Job of role curator and cancels every other open Job as before (`usCuratorJobs`, `usOpenJobs`). `pgClosedCancel(dir, taskID, curator)` takes the hook as an argument, so no Store field was added. When a hook is set and no curator Job is open, `usPostCurator` takes the next Job id (`gkNextJobID`), asks the hook for the body, checks it with `contract.Validate("job-record", …)` and posts it with `s.Feed.Post` under the task lock and lease, as `gkReplan` posts. A hook error, or a body the schema refuses, is one Diagnostics line and posts nothing; the cancels stand. `pgClosedTask` then returns false (go on to the tick) while every open Job is a curator Job, so the tick assigns and spawns it like any Job; otherwise it releases the lease as before.
- `internal/orchestrator/stream.go`: the field `ClosedCurator func(dir, taskID, id string) (map[string]any, error)` after `FirstJob`. Nil means base behaviour.
- `internal/cli/worker.go`: `workerClosedCurator(env)` set on the Stream. It resolves the task, reads the brain queue with `brain.Store{…}.WaitingForTask` (Personal maps to no project, as deliver does), and answers `curatorJobBody(id, T-NN, ids)`, the body `grid deliver` posts, or nil when nothing waits.
- Two new test files: internal/orchestrator/us_closed_curator_test.go and internal/cli/us_close_curator_test.go (rows above).
- Why the stream and not the close: `postCuratorJob` needs a task-lease writer, which a person's close does not hold. The stream holds the lease at this point.

## Open items

- A curator Job that closes without filing (rejected, or cancelled by a person) leaves the proposals waiting; if the stream visits the closed task again, it posts another curator Job. That follows from one-open-curator-Job-at-a-time; a curator that always closes without filing would be re-posted at each visit. Not capped here: the brief asks for no cap. Not tested: no test closes a curator Job unfiled and passes again.
- The stream visits a closed task only while it holds an open Job or the task lease. A closed task with no open Job, no held lease and proposals waiting is not visited, so this change does not curate it. Not tested, and not changed here (outside Touches).
- A running child of a Job a close cancels keeps running until it ends (see the note in pg_closed_task.go); a curator Job a close keeps is not touched.
- The first commit (`38ff14c9`) holds both the pass change and the orchestrator tests: an amend swept the staged test file into it.
