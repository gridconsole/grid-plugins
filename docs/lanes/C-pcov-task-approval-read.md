## What changed

- New `internal/task/approval_read_test.go`. One helper block (`approvalFeed`, `postApproveJob`, `postJobRecord`, `acceptControl`) opens the task Feed with a member writer on the "task" lease at fence 1 (lease file written as a `feed.Lease`, then `Handover`) and posts the Person/approve Job and its accepted control with `feed.Store.Post`. `tick()` sleeps 3 ms between posts so two ULIDs never share a millisecond (the reader picks the greatest id, and ULIDs only order by time across milliseconds).
- 14 tests: the helper check, rows 1 to 5 and 7 (planJobResult, ApprovalFromJob, ApprovalForGate, event), rows 8 and 9.

## Findings

1. The job body for the Feed needs `"id": "<job>"` in the body as well as `Job` on the input. Without it `Post` refuses: `$.body: oneOf matched 0 branches: $.body: missing required ref` (it reads the body as a post). internal/question/job.go:31-55 builds the body without an `id`; its port adds it. The helper adds it. Neither of the two required posts is otherwise refused, and the fold shows the Job accepted, so rows 1 to 4 are not NOT DONE.
2. The Feed accepts a second `accepted` control on a Job that is already accepted, and the fold keeps it accepted. So row 3 is the later-record case, not a pinned refusal.
3. `TestMarkBriefRefusesABriefOverTheReadLimit` already exists in the package; the new `TestMarkBriefSizeLimit` repeats the over-limit case and adds the exact-1-MiB boundary.
