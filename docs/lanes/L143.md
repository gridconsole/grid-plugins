## What changed
- `internal/brain/revert.go`: `Store.Revert(level, topic, name, to, origin)`, `ChangesReadAllowed`, `RevertAllowed`. Publishes version `to` as a new version (stored copy, atomic publish, `reverted` audit line, then a level index rebuild); a no-op when the entry and the newest copy already hold those bytes.
- `internal/braincmd/changes.go`: the two verbs; `command.go`: `Env.Role`, usage text, verb switch. `internal/cli/brain.go` passes `GRID_ROLE` from `env.getenv`.
- `contract/README.md`, `contract/commands.json`, both help goldens, `cli.go` summary, inventory annotations and guest parity rows (plus the early-answer cases for the refusal lines).
- Tests: `internal/brain/revert_test.go`, `internal/braincmd/changes_test.go`, `TestBrainChangesAndRevertByRole` in `internal/cli/brain_test.go`.

## Decisions the brief left open
- `revert --json` prints `{"version": n, "noop": bool}`; plain prints `v<n>` or `already at v<n>`. Superseded by L143R: `noop` means nothing was written, as the brief defines it. The store reports it (`RevertTo`, `Wrote`, decided under the level lock), so a republished stale entry or a restored deleted entry prints `v<n>` and noop false, with no new version when the newest copy already holds the bytes.
- Config topics are refused (their entries are not notes and are edited by their own commands). A copy that does not parse as a valid entry of that topic, name and level, or a name held by another topic, is refused.
- `--limit` passes through as in `Store.Changes` (0 or less: all). Control characters in plain `changes` output print as `?`.

Branch lane-L143R, from lane-L143F (9cde7e71) with main 653340e1 merged (it carries L138, so `brain check` sits beside `changes` and `revert`). ### Review must-fix
`grid brain revert` printed `already at v3` / `{"version":3,"noop":true}` when it had republished a stale or deleted entry, because `runRevert` compared the returned version with a `Store.Versions` read taken before the call and outside the level lock.

### What changed
- `internal/brain/revert.go`: `RevertResult{Entry, Version, Wrote}` and `Store.RevertTo`, which sets `Wrote` under the level lock (true when the entry was published and logged, even if no version was added). `Store.Revert` is a thin wrapper with its old signature.
- `internal/braincmd/changes.go`: `runRevert` drops the `store.Versions` read and prints from `res.Version`, with `noop = !res.Wrote`.
- `contract/README.md` and the "Decisions" line above: `noop` means nothing was written.
- Rows: `TestRevertReportsAWriteWhenItRepublishedTheEntry` (entry holds v2 / entry deleted, text and JSON: `v3` and `{"version":3,"noop":false}`, one new `reverted` audit line, no copy added, then the same request is the real no-op), `TestRevertToTheCurrentVersionWritesNothingAndSaysSo` (noop true, log bytes, audit line count and entry unchanged) in `internal/braincmd/changes_test.go`; `TestRevertToReportsWhetherItWrote` (new version, current bytes, stale entry, missing entry) in `internal/brain/revert_test.go`.
